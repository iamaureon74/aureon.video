aureon — void
a little less noise. a little more possibility.

1. double-click aureon.exe
2. your browser opens the app (it runs only on this computer; nothing is uploaded)
3. drop a video in, click to browse, or paste it with ctrl+v

first run: the transcription model (~800 mb) downloads once. after that it works offline.

windows may say "windows protected your pc" because the app is not code-signed yet.
click "more info" -> "run anyway".

command line (optional), from this folder:
  aureon.exe scan  video.mov
  aureon.exe clean video.mov -o out
